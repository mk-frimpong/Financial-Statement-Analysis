# Three-Statement Financial Analysis Dashboard in Excel

## 1. Project Overview

This project presents an interactive financial analysis dashboard developed in Microsoft Excel. It brings together three core financial statements—the Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow Statement—to demonstrate how financial data can be organized, analyzed, and presented to support business decision-making.

The project uses simulated financial transactions for an illustrative business. It is designed as a portfolio case study demonstrating financial reporting, spreadsheet modeling, data validation, and analytical presentation.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Develop a consolidated view of business financial performance.
- Analyze revenue, expenses, profitability, assets, liabilities, and equity.
- Track cash inflows, outflows, and closing cash balances.
- Use Excel formulas to automate financial calculations.
- Validate financial statements using accounting and reconciliation checks.
- Present financial information in a clear, professional dashboard.

## 4. Dashboard Components

### Income Statement

Summarizes revenue streams, cost of goods sold, gross profit, operating and other expenses, and net profit for the selected reporting period.

### Balance Sheet

Presents current and fixed assets, liabilities, and equity to provide a snapshot of the business's financial position.

### Cash Flow Statement

Organizes cash movements into operating, investing, and financing activities and reconciles opening cash to closing cash.

### Trial Balance

Summarizes debit and credit balances across the accounts and provides a basis for checking the arithmetic integrity of the accounting records.

### Quality Assurance Checks

Includes checks designed to identify formula errors and verify key accounting and cash flow reconciliations.

## 5. Key Findings

For the illustrative 2025 reporting period, the dashboard reports:

| Financial metric | Reported value |
|---|---:|
| Total income | $675,000 |
| Gross profit | $466,500 |
| Net profit | $258,100 |
| Total assets | $1,582,300 |
| Total liabilities and equity | $1,582,300 |
| Net operating cash flow | $352,800 |
| Closing cash balance | $1,119,400 |

The reported net profit margin is approximately **38.2%**, calculated as net profit divided by total income.

The balance sheet balances, and the reported cash flow reconciles opening cash of $738,100 to closing cash of $1,119,400. The trial balance also reports equal debit and credit totals of $4,876,200.

These results demonstrate the importance of reconciling financial statements and validating formulas before presenting financial insights.

## 6. Data Quality and Limitations

The transactions used in this project are simulated and do not represent the actual financial records of a company.

Although the principal reconciliation checks pass, balanced financial statements alone do not establish that every transaction has been correctly classified or that all accounting treatments are appropriate. Further review of transaction postings, opening balances, account mappings, and cash flow classifications would be required before using a model for actual financial reporting.

The figures should therefore be interpreted as illustrative results from a financial analysis exercise rather than audited financial information.
